📍 Philadelphia Mummers Parade on Broad Street, New Year's Day · 👕 Sparky: turquoise sequined Mummers suit with a big ruffled white collar, a turquoise bowler hat with huge pink ostrich feathers and a banjo strapped across his chest · 🐶 Hank: tiny gold sequin cape and a little gold crown with feathers, enthroned on top of the glitter wagon

Sparky joined the Philadelphia Mummers string band because he heard there would be "strutting." There was strutting. There was also a rope, a chain and a banjo he can't really play, and somehow Sparky ended up as the horse for a glitter-covered wagon on Broad Street. Up on the throne, high above the whole parade, sits Hank in his gold sequin cape and a feathered crown, holding a gold parasol and staring into the camera like the Mummers hired him as a king and he is not impressed by the service. Sparky has two bowler-hat feathers in his eye, sequins in his beard and 14 blocks to go, but every time he stops, Hank taps the parasol. The crowd thinks it's part of the act. Hank says tonight's three plays are coming as soon as the strutting speeds up.
