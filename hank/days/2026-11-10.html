St. Louis is 4-2-1 at home and Detroit is 3-3-2 on the road and 7-8-3 overall. Both teams are mediocre, so the edge rests almost entirely on the goalie matchup. That's one signal, so it gets 1u, and it's a moneyline rather than a -1.5 puck line, per the Day 1 lesson. If Gibson is scratched for a better Detroit goalie, the edge shrinks.
