Seventy-Two Steps and One Freeloader

📍 the Rocky Steps of the Philadelphia Museum of Art at sunrise on Black Friday, with the Benjamin Franklin Parkway and the Philadelphia skyline behind · 👕 Sparky: baggy vintage gray hooded sweatsuit with a white towel around his neck, a heather-gray knit watch cap and black canvas high-top sneakers, climbing the steps dead center toward the camera with Hank on his shoulders and an overstuffed Black Friday shopping bag in each hand · 🐶 Hank: tiny shiny royal-blue satin boxing robe with white trim and tiny red boxing gloves, riding on Sparky's shoulders with his gloved paws resting on Sparky's shoulders

Sparky's Black Friday plan was efficient: hit the 5 AM doorbusters, then run the Rocky Steps to burn off yesterday's stuffing. The shopping went fine. The run did not. Somewhere around step 40, Hank, dressed in a royal-blue satin boxing robe and tiny red gloves, decided his legs were finished and climbed onto Sparky's shoulders, where he has been riding like the heavyweight champion of the world ever since. Now Sparky is hauling fifty pounds of bulldog and two overstuffed shopping bags up all 72 steps, red-faced, wheezing and completely deadpan, while Hank grins at the sunrise like he trained for this his whole life. A jogger at the top is already humming the theme song. Hank promises that once they reach the top, he'll throw both gloves in the air and hand down today's three plays, and he expects the cheesesteak at the bottom to count as his treat.
