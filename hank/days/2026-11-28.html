Unchained Malady

📍 a brick-walled community pottery studio in Fishtown, Philadelphia, on a rainy Saturday morning, with shelves of lopsided unfired pots behind them · 👕 Sparky: cream cable-knit fisherman sweater with the sleeves pushed up under a clay-splattered olive canvas work apron, chunky tortoiseshell reading glasses pushed up on his head and a splat of gray clay on his cheek, seated at the pottery wheel facing the camera with both hands on a collapsing vase · 🐶 Hank: tiny navy-blue beret and a clay-smeared blue denim artist's smock, standing up behind Sparky with his muddy front paws draped over Sparky's shoulders and his face peeking over Sparky's shoulder, eyes half closed in a dreamy romantic pose

After Friday's 0-3 card, Sparky signed up for a Saturday-morning pottery class in Fishtown to find some inner calm. He found a lump of wet clay instead. Ten minutes in, his elegant vase started leaning like a drunk at last call, and right on cue Hank, wearing a navy beret and a clay-smeared denim smock, climbed up behind him, draped two muddy paws over his shoulders and pressed his wrinkled face against Sparky's ear, eyes half closed, as if somebody had just cued up "Unchained Melody." The instructor has stopped teaching and started filming. Sparky stares into the camera, completely deadpan, while the vase slowly folds in half under his hands. Hank insists the vase isn't ruined, it's abstract, and he promises that as soon as the wheel stops spinning he'll hand over today's three plays, carved into a slab of clay, of course.
