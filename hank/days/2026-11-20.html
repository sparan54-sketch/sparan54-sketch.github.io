Rolling in the Deep End

📍 log-rolling pond at a north-woods lumberjack festival · 👕 Sparky: gray wool newsboy flat cap, white sleeveless ribbed undershirt, bright red suspenders and rolled-up dark jeans, barefoot · 🐶 Hank: tiny orange knit pom-pom beanie

Ten seconds in, he's got one bare foot in the air, both arms windmilling and his flat cap hanging on for dear life, while the crowd in the bleachers screams like it's the Super Bowl. Hank, meanwhile, is parked on the near end of the same spinning log in his little orange pom-pom beanie, not moving one wrinkle, staring at the camera like he's waiting for a bus. The judge has already stopped the clock out of pity. Hank says he'll hand over tonight's three plays the moment Sparky stops splashing him.
