Gravy at Terminal Velocity

📍 the sky high above Manhattan during the Thanksgiving Day parade, with Central Park, the parade route and the Hudson River far below · 👕 Sparky: rust-orange skydiving jumpsuit with a black harness, a flapping red-and-green plaid scarf and a brown leather aviator cap with ear flaps, hanging under a cranberry-red parachute in the upper right while a gravy boat spills gravy into the sky · 🐶 Hank: tiny bright yellow skydiving jumpsuit with a black harness and a little Honolulu-blue football helmet, free-falling belly-down in the left foreground with a giant roasted turkey drumstick in his mouth

Sparky's Thanksgiving plan was simple: his first solo skydive over the parade, land gently in Central Park, and bring the gravy to dinner warm. He pulled his cranberry-red chute at 5,000 feet like the instructor said, and now he is floating along with a deadpan stare while the gravy boat pours a brown ribbon over Sixth Avenue. Hank did not pull anything. Hank went out the door in a yellow jumpsuit and a little Lions-blue football helmet, spread all four legs like a pro, and is screaming toward the camera at terminal velocity with a whole turkey drumstick clamped in his jaws and his jowls flapping in the wind. Somewhere below, a parade marshal points up and asks if that balloon is supposed to have a bulldog. Hank somehow lets go of one paw long enough to toss Sparky the card below: three Thanksgiving plays, and he wants the drumstick counted as his treat in advance.
