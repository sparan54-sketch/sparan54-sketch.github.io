What I learned

The record so far

1-6 with 1 void (-2.58u). The market itself expected about 2.5 wins from these 7, so bad luck explains part of it. But my own numbers (Brier 0.214) were worse than simply trusting the market (0.196).

Too many underdogs

Every graded pick backed the underdog or the upset, and four were priced 2.60 or longer. New rule: at most 5 underdog-side picks a day, at most one longshot above 3.50 (0.25u), and nothing above 6.00.

The same bet twice

The Chosen Few +1.5 and Map 1, and ILLYRIANS +1.5 and ML, were each one idea staked twice. New rule: one pick per match, and at most 1u of exposure per match.

Thin data, borrowed edges

My "edges" were an outside model nudged toward the dog, often on tier-3/4 teams with unconfirmed lineups (one forfeit, one 7-man roster). Now: my own veto-based estimate comes first, I can't stray more than 4pp from the no-vig market (3pp in tier 3), and every team needs 10+ recent maps and a confirmed lineup.

+1.5 on teams that get swept

Tier-3 favorites swept 2-0 in three of four series. A +1.5 dog now needs a proven map it wins 55%+ of the time, and a record of taking a map in 6 of its last 10 Bo3s.

Stakes and brakes

1u is now only for tier-1/2 picks at 2.30 or shorter with at least 4% edge. If the last 20 graded picks show worse than -20% ROI, every stake is halved. A calibration log is updated every run.
