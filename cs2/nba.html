Fri Oct 304 lessons

Duel's ladders are smooth, real counts are lumpy

On low-count stats (rebounds or assists around 4-5), Duel prices its ladder as if the stat were smooth. The rung one step below the main line comes out much cheaper than at books that price the real, lumpy distribution: Murray 4+ rebounds is 1.69 at Duel vs -185 to -225 everywhere else. Check the rungs next to the main line against other books' alt ladders before calling a Duel market "all fails".
